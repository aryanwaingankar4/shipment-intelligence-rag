=== INCIDENT REPORT FY2024 — TEMPERATURE BREACHES ===
Document ID: DOC-IR-2024 | Version: 1.0 | Prepared by: QA Department, TraceLink BioMed.
Scope: Root cause analysis of the two temperature breaches recorded in FY2024.

=== INCIDENT INC-2024-01 — SH-2024-003 QUARANTINE ===
Related shipment: SH-2024-003 | Product: TL-COLD-9001 | Batch: BATCH-CRY-2401
Event: Cold chain consignment to Fortis Hospital Hyderabad reached 11.2°C, exceeding the 10°C limit for 3 hours.
Detection: Data logger alarm flagged on arrival scan, Feb 7.
Root cause: PCM cold packs were not fully conditioned (frozen) before loading; transit took 4 hours longer than planned due to a highway diversion.
Contributing factor: No backup cold box was available at the transfer point.
Immediate action: Consignment quarantined, QA notified within 2 hours, deviation DEV-2024-003 raised.
Disposition: Pending QA stability assessment; product not released.
CAPA: Mandatory 24-hour PCM conditioning checklist before loading; add GPS route alerts for cold chain trucks.
Status: CAPA in progress, target closure Q3 FY2024.

=== INCIDENT INC-2024-02 — SH-2024-007 REJECTION ===
Related shipment: SH-2024-007 | Product: TL-FROZ-7700 | Batch: BATCH-FRZ-2403
Event: Frozen biological consignment to SciLab Research Kolkata rose to -8°C, above the -10°C automatic rejection threshold.
Detection: Logger review on arrival, Apr 9.
Root cause: Insufficient dry ice loaded for a 2-day transit; dry ice fully sublimated before delivery.
Contributing factor: Vendor ArcticLine used a standard insulated case instead of the specified extended-duration case.
Immediate action: Full batch rejected automatically, destruction record DES-2024-007 raised, CDSCO notified within 72 hours.
Disposition: Destroyed per protocol; no product released.
CAPA: Dry ice quantity formula revised to 1.5x transit hours; mandatory extended-duration cases for transit over 24 hours; ArcticLine corrective action audit scheduled.
Status: CAPA open, vendor audit pending.

=== INCIDENT TREND ANALYSIS FY2024 ===
Both breaches involved temperature-sensitive products on multi-day transit.
Common theme: thermal packaging not matched to actual transit duration.
Breach rate fell within tolerance after CAPA rollout in the second half of the year.
Recommendation: Pre-shipment thermal validation for any cold chain or frozen route exceeding 24 hours.
