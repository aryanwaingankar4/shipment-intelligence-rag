=== GDP COMPLIANCE GUIDE — TRACELINK BIOMED ===
Document ID: DOC-CG-2024 | Version: 4.0 | Based on WHO GDP and CDSCO guidelines.

=== SECTION 1 — SHIPMENT DOCUMENTATION ===
A signed Delivery Order is mandatory before any dispatch.
The shipment log must be updated within 24 hours of dispatch.
Every shipment must include: tax Invoice, Certificate of Analysis (CoA), temperature log, and batch/expiry information.
Temperature data loggers must be activated at origin before loading.
For refrigerated items the driver must sign the Cold Chain Handover Form.
A Delivery Order without QA sign-off is treated as void.

=== SECTION 2 — TEMPERATURE MONITORING ===
Cold chain products (2-8°C): if temperature exceeds 10°C at any point, quarantine immediately, notify QA within 2 hours, and do not release until the QA investigation is complete.
Frozen products (-20°C or below): if temperature rises above -10°C, the consignment is automatically rejected and a destruction record must be raised within 48 hours.
Ambient products (15-25°C): if temperature exceeds 30°C for 4 or more continuous hours, report to the warehouse supervisor and QA issues a deviation report.
All temperature loggers must be calibrated every 6 months by an accredited lab.
Logger calibration certificates are retained for 3 years.

=== SECTION 3 — WAREHOUSE COMPLIANCE ===
Cold storage units are validated annually using IQ, OQ, and PQ protocols.
Pest control is performed monthly; inventory reconciliation is performed quarterly.
FIFO (First In First Out) is mandatory for all product storage.
Rejected or expired stock is moved to a segregated quarantine zone labelled DO NOT USE.
All warehouse staff complete GDP refresher training annually.
Temperature mapping of each cold room is repeated every 2 years.

=== SECTION 4 — SHIPMENT DELAY PROTOCOL ===
For any delay exceeding 48 hours, QA must reassess product integrity before acceptance.
For delayed cold chain shipments, temperature logs must be reviewed before acceptance.
The customer must be notified of any delay exceeding 24 hours.
If a delay is caused by vehicle breakdown, a backup validated vehicle must be dispatched within 6 hours.

=== SECTION 5 — RETURNS AND RECALLS ===
Returns are accepted only within 30 days of delivery.
Returned products are quarantined and cannot be restocked without QA approval.
In a recall, affected batches must be identified within 4 hours.
The distribution list is retrieved from the TraceLink traceability system.
CDSCO must be notified within 72 hours of a confirmed recall.

=== SECTION 6 — PENALTIES ===
Shipment log not updated within 24 hours: Warning plus Rs. 5,000 penalty.
Releasing product without QA clearance after a breach: license suspension.
Falsifying temperature records: termination plus legal action.
Repeated GDP training non-completion: removal from cold chain duties.
